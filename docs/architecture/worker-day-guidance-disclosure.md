# Worker Day guidance disclosure

Worker Day remains a delivery surface, not a prose-authoring authority.

The worker list may show the task title. When the worker opens a task, the drawer may additionally show only guidance that was explicitly placed on that worker delivery projection in `worker_week_projection.delivery_payload.details`.

Those details are presentation guidance. They must not establish operational meaning, create new work semantics, change responsibility, or substitute for governed structured work. Canonical Company Work remains authoritative for the work itself.

The disclosure boundary is intentionally narrow:

- `delivery_payload.details` may be shown to the worker because it was explicitly authored for that delivery.
- `work_items.instructions`, source-work prose, arbitrary metadata, and internal planning evidence are not automatically exposed through Worker Day.
- Delivery details preserve their authored order and are shown only with the task they belong to.
- Completion, attention, acceptance, and authority rules are unchanged by displaying guidance.

If richer worker guidance is needed later, it must be added through an explicit governed delivery contract rather than by leaking canonical or internal prose into the employee surface.
