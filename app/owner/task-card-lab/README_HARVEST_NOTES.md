# Thursday Harvest specimen notes

- This Task Card Lab specimen is fixture-only.
- Thursday Harvest is a collection/route card assembled from plantings that are inside, entering, or repeating within the harvest window for the week.
- Each bed resolves independently: Harvested, Nothing ready, Left for later, or Log it.
- Harvest quantities belong to the underlying planting/bed history, not to a generic Thursday note.
- A bed may appear on the Thursday route without requiring a cut that day.
- The finish action means the harvest round was checked, not that every eligible bed produced a harvest.
